Privacy Policy - IMC Simples

Developer shown on Google Play: Simei Alves
Responsible developer: Simei Pasqualeto Alves (Portugal)
Privacy contact: geral.simei@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to IMC Simples, developed and provided by Simei Alves (Simei Pasqualeto Alves). It explains how information is handled by the app, including its Google sign-in, purchase, advertising and analytics services. You can calculate your body mass index (BMI) without signing in.

1. BMI calculations and information stored on your device

Height and weight that you enter are used on your device to calculate BMI. When you choose to save a result, the app stores the BMI, weight, measurement unit and date in its local history. Language and measurement-unit preferences are also stored locally. The app does not upload this BMI history to Firebase or send the height, weight or BMI values to the advertising or analytics services described below.

2. Optional Google sign-in and purchases

If you use Google sign-in for the option to remove advertisements, Google and Firebase Authentication process your sign-in information. This can include your account identifier, email address and profile information supplied by Google, as well as technical information such as IP address and user agent for authentication and security. We do not receive your Google password.

The app uses Cloud Firestore to store a user identifier and whether advertisements have been removed. A purchase timestamp may also be stored. This information is used to provide and restore the ad-removal entitlement. Google Play processes in-app payments; the app receives purchase or entitlement information, rather than your payment-card details.

3. Advertising, analytics and other service providers

  • Google AdMob: the app uses Google's advertising service to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and advertisement interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers, including an advertising ID where available. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This information is not necessarily anonymous.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: analytics is initialized on supported devices and environments to understand app usage and improve its performance. It can process usage events, app or device information and identifiers according to Google's service settings and policies.
  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore: Google processes account and entitlement information to provide the optional sign-in and purchase features described above.
  • Google Fonts: the app loads fonts from Google's servers, which involves transmitting technical request information, including the IP address.
  • GitHub Pages: this privacy-policy website is hosted by GitHub. Visiting the website involves technical requests to GitHub, separately from using the BMI calculator.

Information is provided to these services to operate the features described above. Their processing and retention practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, Google Mobile Ads data disclosures and GitHub Privacy Statement. These providers may process information outside your country.

4. Data retention and deletion requests

Local BMI history: saved results remain in the app's local storage until you delete the individual entries from History or clear the app's storage through your device settings. Clearing the app's storage also removes its saved language and unit preferences.

Account and entitlement data: Firebase account and purchase-entitlement records are retained to support sign-in and the ad-removal feature until deletion is requested, subject to any retention required by applicable law. Clearing the app's local storage does not delete these cloud records.

To request deletion of your IMC Simples app account and associated data, or to request access to or correction of your information, email geral.simei@gmail.com with the subject IMC Simples - account and data deletion and identify the email address used to sign in. You can make this request without reinstalling the app. Do not send your password or payment-card details. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account before acting on it.

This request concerns the IMC Simples account and associated records; it does not delete your Google account or Google's independently maintained payment records. Advertising and analytics information is retained and deleted according to the relevant Google service settings and policies. Android provides controls for the advertising ID, and Google provides account and advertising privacy controls.

5. Data security

The Google services described above use encrypted network connections. BMI history is stored in the app's local storage and depends on the security of your device. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through IMC Simples, contact the responsible developer at geral.simei@gmail.com so that the information and any deletion request can be reviewed.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
